## Source package evidence

`tests/source_package.sh` creates its tarball from an explicit, ordered
filesystem manifest using `tar`, not Git and not the whole checkout. It excludes
nested `.git`/`.cortexkit`, Cargo target output, prebuilt binaries, staging
temporaries, `.DS_Store`, and AppleDouble files; recursively verifies those
exclusions after extraction; fingerprints the manifest before/after; and records
the original generated-output paths, sizes, and mtimes without hashing their
contents. Failing/logging `git`, `curl`, `wget`, and `nc` sentinels are first on
`PATH` for the extracted build, plugin load, install, and uninstall and remain
uninvoked. The extracted path contains spaces and shell metacharacters. Its
offline build uses only the pre-existing local Cargo cache, its no-argument
binary exits 1 with `Error: expected client_tty session_id pane_id`, and the
real isolated two-client tmux route executes the extracted shim, launcher,
wrapper, and built binary with exact invoking tty/session/pane argv. The staged
uninstall preserves its sibling and destination directory.
