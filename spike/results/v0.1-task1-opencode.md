## Registry and cache investigation

The temporary package's `npm pack` cache was separate from the clean-room npm
cache consumed by OpenCode. With scoped `.npmrc` entries pointing only at the
loopback registry, OpenCode 1.17.20 requested exactly two metadata and two
tarball routes, once each:

```text
/@opencode-ai%2fplugin
/%40opencode-ai%2Fplugin/-/plugin-1.17.20.tgz
/@xiopt%2fpane-dash-opencode
/%40xiopt%2Fpane-dash-opencode/-/pane-dash-opencode-0.1.0.tgz
```

The prior missing-tarball conclusion was incorrect: the observed misses were
OpenCode npm-cache hits across the bootstrap/observation runs. The companion
metadata uses the matching `1.17.20` version and the packed plugin exposes both
`.` and `./server` from `dist/index.js`. `audit=false` and `fund=false` prevent
non-package npm routes; any extra or public route fails the spike.
